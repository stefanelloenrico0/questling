## Full description

Questling isn't a to-do list with points: it's a travel companion.

Take a short quiz (eight light questions, nothing serious) and you're matched with one of eight heroes (and three more unlock as you grow: a pirate, a philosopher slime and a little dragon), each with a voice, a story and a personality. You can switch whenever you like: your level stays yours.

• **Quests made for you**: daily, weekly and monthly goals. Every completed quest gives experience and levels you up.
• **A hero with character**: from the solemn knight to the gentle cleric, from the theatrical bard to the arcade-cabinet robot. Reminders arrive in their voice, with jokes, never as scolding.
• **A story in chapters**: unlock it by levelling up.
• **Streaks with shields**: a skipped day doesn't reset everything if you have a shield.
• **Stats and achievements**: the heatmap of the last 12 weeks and your records.
• **Your data stays yours**: it works offline and without an account. No ads, no tracking. If you want, you can create a free account to have your data on several devices; you can delete it at any time from the app.
• **Your own backup**: export and import your data to a file.
• **Italian and English**: the whole app, including your hero's messages.

Notifications are reminders scheduled on your phone (at most two a day, never at night) and you manage them from the settings.
